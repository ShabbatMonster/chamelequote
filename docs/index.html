    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does it work?
    All of the coin's liquidity sits in one Orca pool owned by an on-chain program. Nobody holds a key to it. The token it trades against, the quote, can be changed: stocks like TSLAX, cash like USDC, or SOL and other coins from the approved list.
    How do I change the quote?
    Pick a token above and burn 1,000,000. The program pulls the liquidity, swaps the backing into your pick, and puts it back in a new pool at the same dollar price, minus swap fees (usually under 1%). It takes a few transactions, about a minute, and trading pauses until they are done. If a switch is not finished within 10 minutes, anyone can cancel it and your tokens come back.
    Why can't I switch right now?
    Only one switch runs at a time. After a big price move the program also waits until its average price catches up (up to about half an hour), so nobody can pump the price and switch at a bad rate.
    How do I rename it?
    Type a name, ticker and picture and burn 1,000,000. The picture is stored on IPFS. Wallets and Solscan show the change within minutes; some sites like DexScreener cache names for longer.
    Is it safe?
    The program has not been audited yet. Stock tokens are issued by a company that can freeze or move them, and that includes the coin's backing while it is held in one. Only burn what you are happy to burn.
